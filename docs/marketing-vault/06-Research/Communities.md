## (a) Hacker News

### Show HN rules (official)

Source: [Show HN Guidelines](https://news.ycombinator.com/showhn.html)

- **On-topic:** Things people can run on their computers — software, apps, hardware with video.
- **Off-topic:** Blog posts, sign-up pages, newsletters, lists, landing pages, waitlists, fundraisers.
- Title must start with **`Show HN:`**; you must have built it and be present to discuss.
- Product should be **non-trivial**, personally meaningful, tryable **without signup/email gate** when possible.
- **Do not** ask friends to upvote or comment.
- New features / patch releases generally **not** substantive enough; major overhauls OK.
- Comments: no generated/AI-edited text per [HN Guidelines](https://news.ycombinator.com/newsguidelines.html).

**Kraken fit:** Strong if free 30-min trial requires no signup (matches HN preference). Frame as dev tool / interview prep assistant, not "cheat tool."

### Best posting times

Community guidance (multiple 2025–2026 analyses; not official):

| window | rationale |
|--------|-----------|
| **Tue–Thu, 8–10 AM ET** (13:00–15:00 UTC) | Peak US engineer traffic; early velocity matters for front page |
| **Mon 00:00 UTC** (Sun 7 PM ET) | Lower competition in some datasets; higher breakout variance |
| Avoid Fri PM / Sat | Lower engagement for Show HN discovery |

Plan to stay online **2+ hours** after posting; first-hour comment velocity (~8–12 points) correlates with visibility.

### Launch sequencing (recommended)

1. **Week 0:** SaaSHub + AlternativeTo + TAAFT (comparison + AI discovery)
2. **Week 1:** Show HN (if try-without-signup ready) **or** Product Hunt (separate day)
3. **Week 2:** Futurepedia, Futurepedia-tier long tail, Indie Hackers build story
4. **Ongoing:** Competitor alternative pages on own site → internal link to directories

Prepare **one submission kit** (name, URL, 60-char tagline, 250-char blurb, long description, 512px logo, 3–5 screenshots, pricing) and reuse across all forms.
