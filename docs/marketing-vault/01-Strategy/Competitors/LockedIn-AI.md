## Weaknesses / angles Kraken can use

- **Complex, metered pricing** — Credit plans charge per minute; thinking about credits mid-interview vs Kraken flat unlimited plans
- **Stealth paywalled** — Cheapest credit monthly ($69.99) excludes desktop app and stealth (support FAQ); Kraken includes invisibility in all paid tiers
- **Account/signup friction** — Requires account creation; Kraken trial needs no signup
- **Higher effective monthly on many tiers** — Credit monthly $69.99 vs Kraken $54; unlimited promos require verifying current discounts
- **Lifetime price gap** — ~$1,499 lifetime (unverified) vs Kraken $684
- **Process/detection concerns** — Independent reviewers report desktop window detectable; extension visible in browser (https://interviewsidekick.com — cited in Interview Coder vs LockedIn comparison)
- **116ms claim misleading** — Mirly latency benchmark notes LockedIn publishes LLM-step-only latency, not end-to-end (https://www.mirly.co.uk/blog/latency-teardown-6-copilots)
