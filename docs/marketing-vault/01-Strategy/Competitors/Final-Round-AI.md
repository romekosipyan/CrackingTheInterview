## Weaknesses / angles Kraken can use

- **Higher monthly price** — $90/mo month-to-month vs Kraken $54/mo; even annual effective $25/mo requires $300 upfront
- **No lifetime option** — Subscription-only vs Kraken $684 lifetime
- **No weekly plan** — Kraken $24/week for short sprints
- **Limited free trial** — 10-minute one-time trial vs Kraken 30-minute device trial, no signup
- **Monthly plan non-refundable** — Per official pricing blog; billing disputes reported on Trustpilot (https://hiredradar.com/final-round-ai-review/)
- **Latency concerns** — Mirly benchmark: p50 1,810ms, p95 2,940ms — slowest of 6 copilots tested (https://www.mirly.co.uk/blog/latency-teardown-6-copilots)
- **No Mermaid system-design output** — Text solutions in panel vs Kraken native diagram generation
