- **5–6× higher monthly price** — $299/mo vs Kraken $54/mo; even lifetime $799 vs Kraken $684
- **No weekly plan** — Kraken's $24/week fits short interview sprints
- **No free AI trial** — Free tier is installer-only; Kraken offers 30-minute live trial without signup
- **Behavioral/system-design gap** — Independent reviews note coding-only focus; no STAR scaffolding (https://interviewsidekick.com/blog/interview-coder-review)
